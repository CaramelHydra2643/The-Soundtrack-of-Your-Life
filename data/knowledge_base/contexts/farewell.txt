CONTEXT: Farewell

DESCRIPTION:
Farewell moments involve saying goodbye, whether temporary or permanent. These experiences often carry mixed emotions - sadness at parting combined with gratitude for shared experiences and hope for the future.

EMOTIONAL ASSOCIATIONS:
- Nostalgia and longing
- Sadness and melancholy
- Gratitude and appreciation
- Hope and optimism (for future)
- Bittersweet feelings
- Love and connection

MUSICAL QUALITIES:
- Emotional, heartfelt melodies
- Often bittersweet tone
- Moderate, reflective tempo
- Gentle yet emotionally powerful
- May build to emotional climax
- Emphasis on melody and lyrics

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Indie folk
- Singer-songwriter
- Ballads
- Acoustic pop
- Soft rock
- Contemporary folk

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Piano (emotional centerpiece)
- Acoustic guitar
- Strings (violin, cello)
- Light percussion
- Vocal harmonies
- Minimal bass

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Slow to moderate tempo
- Low to moderate energy
- Gentle dynamics with emotional peaks

RELATED THEMES:
- Graduation and leaving school
- Moving away
- End of relationships
- Life transitions
- Departure of loved ones
- Closing chapters

COMMON SCENARIOS:
- Last day at college
- Moving to new city
- Friends parting ways
- End of an era
- Temporary separations
- Final goodbyes
