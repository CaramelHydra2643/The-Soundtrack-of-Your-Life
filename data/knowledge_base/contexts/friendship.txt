CONTEXT: Friendship

DESCRIPTION:
Friendship encompasses the bonds, experiences, and emotions associated with close platonic relationships. Music capturing friendship often celebrates camaraderie, shared experiences, loyalty, and the joy of companionship.

EMOTIONAL ASSOCIATIONS:
- Happiness and joy
- Gratitude and appreciation
- Nostalgia (when reflecting on memories)
- Warmth and comfort
- Support and loyalty
- Sometimes bittersweetness (when separated)

MUSICAL QUALITIES:
- Uplifting and warm melodies
- Often group vocals or harmonies
- Celebratory or reflective tone
- Moderate to upbeat tempo
- Inclusive, accessible sound

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Folk
- Pop
- Indie pop
- Rock
- Singer-songwriter
- Acoustic

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Acoustic guitar
- Piano
- Group vocals/harmonies
- Upbeat percussion
- Strings (for emotional depth)

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Varies: can be energetic (celebrations) or moderate (reflections)
- Generally positive energy

RELATED THEMES:
- College and school memories
- Shared adventures
- Support through difficulties
- Growing up together
- Farewell and reunion
- Loyalty and trust

COMMON SCENARIOS:
- Graduation
- Road trips
- Group gatherings
- Saying goodbye
- Celebrating together
- Reminiscing
