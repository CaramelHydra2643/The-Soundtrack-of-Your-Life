CONTEXT: Life Transitions

DESCRIPTION:
Life transitions encompass significant changes and turning points - moving between life phases, changing environments, or experiencing personal transformation. These moments often involve complex, mixed emotions.

EMOTIONAL ASSOCIATIONS:
- Excitement and anticipation
- Anxiety and uncertainty
- Nostalgia for what's ending
- Hope for what's beginning
- Reflection and introspection
- Growth and transformation

MUSICAL QUALITIES:
- Dynamic, evolving arrangements
- Often builds from quiet to powerful
- May shift moods within the piece
- Reflective yet forward-looking
- Emotional depth and complexity

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Indie folk
- Alternative rock
- Singer-songwriter
- Cinematic
- Progressive rock
- Art pop

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Piano and/or guitar (foundation)
- Strings (emotional swells)
- Gradually building percussion
- Synthesizers (atmosphere)
- Layered vocals

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Often starts slow, may build
- Variable energy reflecting change
- Dynamic contrasts

RELATED THEMES:
- Coming of age
- Career changes
- Moving to new places
- Personal growth
- Endings and new beginnings
- Self-discovery

COMMON SCENARIOS:
- Graduating
- Starting new jobs
- Leaving home
- Major life decisions
- Relationship changes
- Personal transformations
