GENRE: Classical

DESCRIPTION:
Classical music encompasses Western art music from various periods, characterized by complex compositions, orchestral arrangements, and emphasis on instrumental virtuosity and emotional expression through sophisticated harmonic and melodic structures.

KEY CHARACTERISTICS:
- Complex harmonic structures
- Orchestral arrangements
- Dynamic range and contrast
- Formal compositional techniques
- Emphasis on instrumental performance
- Rich emotional expression

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Full orchestra (strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion)
- Piano (solo or concerto)
- String quartet
- Chamber ensembles
- Choir (in some works)

COMMON MOODS:
- Wide emotional range
- Reflection and contemplation
- Drama and intensity
- Peace and serenity
- Triumph and celebration
- Melancholy and longing

TEMPO RANGE:
- Highly variable (Largo to Presto)
- Often changes within a single piece

ENERGY LEVEL:
- Varies from very low to very high
- Dynamic contrasts are fundamental

POPULAR CONTEXTS:
- Emotional depth and complexity
- Cinematic and dramatic moments
- Meditation and reflection
- Celebration and ceremony
- Intellectual engagement
- Historical and cultural appreciation

RELATED GENRES:
- Neoclassical
- Contemporary classical
- Film scores
- Orchestral pop

NOTABLE FEATURES:
- No vocals in most instrumental works
- Complex arrangements
- Timeless quality
- Emotional sophistication
