GENRE: Indie Folk

DESCRIPTION:
Indie folk combines the independent music ethos with traditional folk instrumentation and storytelling. It often features acoustic instruments, introspective lyrics, and organic production, emphasizing authenticity and emotional expression.

KEY CHARACTERISTICS:
- Acoustic-driven arrangements
- Introspective, personal lyrics
- Organic, natural sound
- Often features unconventional song structures
- Emphasis on melody and harmony
- May incorporate elements from other genres

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Acoustic guitar
- Banjo
- Mandolin
- Piano
- Violin/fiddle
- Light percussion (tambourine, shakers)
- Harmonica
- Upright bass

COMMON MOODS:
- Nostalgia
- Reflection
- Melancholy
- Hope
- Bittersweet emotions
- Peace
- Gratitude

TEMPO RANGE:
- Typically slow to moderate (60-110 BPM)
- Can vary from ballads to uptempo

ENERGY LEVEL:
- Low to moderate
- Focuses on intimacy over intensity

POPULAR CONTEXTS:
- Personal storytelling
- Life transitions
- Nature and rural themes
- Relationships
- Coming of age
- Social commentary

RELATED GENRES:
- Folk
- Singer-songwriter
- Americana
- Alt-country

NOTABLE FEATURES:
- Intimate vocal delivery
- Acoustic authenticity
- Lyrical depth
- Often lo-fi or minimalist production
