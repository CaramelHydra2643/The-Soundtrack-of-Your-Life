GENRE: Ambient

DESCRIPTION:
Ambient music emphasizes tone and atmosphere over traditional musical structure or rhythm. It creates immersive soundscapes designed to induce calm, contemplation, or enhance the environment without demanding focused attention.

KEY CHARACTERISTICS:
- Atmospheric textures and soundscapes
- Minimal or no rhythmic structure
- Long, sustained tones
- Emphasis on timbre and space
- Often lacks conventional melody
- Slowly evolving sound

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Synthesizers (pads, drones)
- Electronic sound design
- Field recordings
- Processed acoustic instruments
- Piano (sparse, ambient)
- Guitar (with heavy effects)

COMMON MOODS:
- Peace and calm
- Contemplation
- Mystery
- Melancholy
- Serenity
- Transcendence

TEMPO RANGE:
- Very slow or tempo-less
- Focus on sustained sounds rather than beats

ENERGY LEVEL:
- Very low to low
- Subtle and unobtrusive

POPULAR CONTEXTS:
- Meditation and relaxation
- Background music
- Sleep and rest
- Focus and concentration
- Emotional processing
- Creating atmosphere

RELATED GENRES:
- New age
- Drone
- Minimal techno
- Post-rock (ambient sections)
- Chillout

NOTABLE FEATURES:
- Non-intrusive design
- Spatial quality
- Repetition and gradual change
- Often instrumental/electronic
