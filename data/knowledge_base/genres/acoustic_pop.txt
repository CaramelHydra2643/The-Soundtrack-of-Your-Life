GENRE: Acoustic Pop

DESCRIPTION:
Acoustic pop blends the accessibility and melody of pop music with acoustic instrumentation. It maintains pop's catchiness while offering a more organic, stripped-down sound that emphasizes vocals and natural instruments.

KEY CHARACTERISTICS:
- Catchy, memorable melodies
- Acoustic instrumentation
- Clear vocal focus
- Radio-friendly structure
- Warm, polished production
- Balance between intimacy and accessibility

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Acoustic guitar (primary)
- Piano
- Light percussion (cajón, brushes)
- Strings (occasional)
- Minimal bass
- Hand claps, finger snaps

COMMON MOODS:
- Happiness
- Nostalgia
- Love and romance
- Optimism
- Bittersweet emotions
- Warmth

TEMPO RANGE:
- Moderate (80-120 BPM)
- Versatile, from ballads to uptempo

ENERGY LEVEL:
- Moderate
- Bright and uplifting
- More energetic than folk, less than electric pop

POPULAR CONTEXTS:
- Relationships
- Personal growth
- Everyday experiences
- Celebrations
- Reflective moments
- Coffee shop ambiance

RELATED GENRES:
- Pop
- Folk pop
- Singer-songwriter
- Indie pop

NOTABLE FEATURES:
- Emphasis on vocal melody
- Clean, crisp production
- Accessible lyrics
- Organic yet polished sound
