EMOTION: Gratitude

DESCRIPTION:
Gratitude is a warm feeling of appreciation and thankfulness. It combines contentment with recognition of positive experiences, people, or circumstances in one's life.

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Warm, full instrumentation
- Major keys with rich harmonies
- Gentle to moderate tempo
- Melodic and heartfelt
- Often features uplifting crescendos

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Folk
- Gospel
- Soul
- Acoustic pop
- Americana
- Singer-songwriter

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Acoustic guitar
- Piano
- Strings
- Harmonica
- Organ
- Vocal harmonies

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Tempo: Moderate (80-110 BPM)
- Energy: Moderate
- Dynamics: Warm, building

ATMOSPHERE:
- Warm and embracing
- Heartfelt and sincere
- Peaceful and content
- Uplifting without being overly energetic
- Reflective appreciation

RELEVANT CONTEXTS:
- Moments of appreciation
- Family gatherings
- Thanksgiving and celebrations
- Reflecting on blessings
- Recognizing support from others
- Life milestones

CAVEATS:
- Gratitude can be quiet or celebratory
- Often mixed with other emotions like nostalgia or joy
