EMOTION: Excitement

DESCRIPTION:
Excitement is a feeling of great enthusiasm and eagerness. It's characterized by heightened energy, anticipation, and positive arousal, often in response to upcoming events or experiences.

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Fast tempo
- Driving rhythms
- Energetic instrumentation
- Dynamic changes and builds
- Major keys
- Uplifting melodies

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Rock
- Pop
- Electronic dance music
- Indie rock
- Pop punk
- Upbeat folk

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Electric guitar (energetic)
- Drums (prominent)
- Bass (driving)
- Synthesizers (bright, pulsing)
- Brass (energetic)
- Energetic vocals

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Tempo: Fast (120-160+ BPM)
- Energy: High to very high
- Dynamics: Powerful, building

ATMOSPHERE:
- Energizing and invigorating
- Uplifting and motivating
- Celebratory
- Anticipatory
- Intense and passionate

RELEVANT CONTEXTS:
- Anticipation of events
- Adventure and exploration
- Celebrations
- Achieving goals
- New opportunities
- Competitive activities

CAVEATS:
- Excitement can vary in intensity
- Can be focused or chaotic
- May overlap with anxiety in some contexts
