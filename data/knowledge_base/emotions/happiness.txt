EMOTION: Happiness

DESCRIPTION:
Happiness is a state of well-being characterized by emotions ranging from contentment to intense joy. In music, it manifests through uplifting melodies, major keys, and energetic rhythms that inspire positive feelings.

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Major key tonality
- Uplifting, bright melodies
- Clear, crisp production
- Rhythmic and danceable elements
- Positive lyrical themes

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Pop
- Indie pop
- Folk pop
- Dance
- Reggae
- Soul
- Funk

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Bright acoustic guitar
- Uplifting piano
- Brass section (trumpet, trombone)
- Hand percussion
- Ukulele
- Synthesizers (bright pads)

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Tempo: Moderate to fast (100-140 BPM)
- Energy: Moderate to high
- Dynamics: Uplifting, energetic

ATMOSPHERE:
- Bright and cheerful
- Optimistic and hopeful
- Celebratory
- Light-hearted
- Energizing

RELEVANT CONTEXTS:
- Celebrations and parties
- Achievements
- Time with loved ones
- Positive life changes
- Moments of success
- Joyful experiences

CAVEATS:
- Happiness can be expressed quietly or energetically
- Not all happy music is fast or loud
- Contentment differs from excitement
