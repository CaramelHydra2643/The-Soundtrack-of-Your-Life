EMOTION: Nostalgia

DESCRIPTION:
Nostalgia is a sentimental longing or wistful affection for the past, typically for a period or place with happy personal associations. It often combines happiness with a tinge of sadness, creating a bittersweet emotional experience.

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Often features warm, organic instrumentation
- Acoustic instruments like guitar and piano are common
- Moderate tempo that allows reflection
- Melodic and harmony-focused rather than rhythm-heavy
- May include vintage or analog production techniques

RECOMMENDED GENRES:
- Indie folk
- Acoustic pop
- Soft rock
- Singer-songwriter
- Classic rock
- Orchestral pop

TYPICAL INSTRUMENTS:
- Acoustic guitar
- Piano
- Strings (violin, cello)
- Light percussion
- Harmonica
- Mellow electric guitar

TEMPO AND ENERGY:
- Tempo: Slow to moderate (60-110 BPM)
- Energy: Low to moderate
- Dynamics: Gentle, gradual builds

ATMOSPHERE:
- Warm and intimate
- Reflective and contemplative
- Bittersweet and wistful
- Comforting yet melancholic

RELEVANT CONTEXTS:
- Memories of childhood
- College and school days
- Past relationships
- Places from the past
- Life transitions
- Farewell moments

CAVEATS:
- Not all nostalgic music is sad
- Can range from joyful reminiscence to melancholic longing
- Cultural and personal experiences shape nostalgic responses
