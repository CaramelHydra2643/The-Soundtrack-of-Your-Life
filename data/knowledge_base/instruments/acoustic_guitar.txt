INSTRUMENT: Acoustic Guitar

DESCRIPTION:
The acoustic guitar is a string instrument that produces sound acoustically through vibration of strings and resonance of the body. It's fundamental to folk, country, singer-songwriter, and many other genres.

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Warm, organic tone
- Percussive and melodic capabilities
- Versatile playing techniques (strumming, fingerpicking)
- Natural resonance
- Portable and intimate

EMOTIONAL RANGE:
- Warmth and comfort
- Nostalgia and reflection
- Joy and celebration (upbeat strumming)
- Melancholy (fingerpicking, minor keys)
- Authenticity and honesty

COMMON PLAYING STYLES:
- Fingerpicking (delicate, intricate)
- Strumming (rhythmic, driving)
- Percussive (body percussion, tapping)
- Slide guitar (emotional, vocal-like)
- Classical (precise, melodic)

SUITED FOR GENRES:
- Folk and indie folk
- Singer-songwriter
- Country and Americana
- Acoustic pop
- Blues
- Flamenco

TYPICAL MOODS:
- Intimate and personal
- Earthy and grounded
- Reflective
- Warm and inviting
- Authentic

COMBINATION INSTRUMENTS:
- Works well with: vocals, piano, light percussion, harmonica
- Often the foundation of acoustic arrangements

CONTEXTUAL USE:
- Storytelling and personal narratives
- Campfire and intimate settings
- Folk traditions
- Contemporary singer-songwriter
- Unplugged performances
