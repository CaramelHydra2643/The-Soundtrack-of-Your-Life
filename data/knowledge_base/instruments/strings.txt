INSTRUMENT: Strings (Violin, Cello, Viola)

DESCRIPTION:
String instruments (violin, viola, cello, double bass) produce sound through bowed or plucked vibrating strings. They're capable of deeply emotional, vocal-like expression and are central to classical and cinematic music.

MUSICAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Warm, rich tones
- Wide dynamic range
- Sustained notes with natural vibrato
- Human voice-like expressiveness
- Can create lush textures when layered

EMOTIONAL RANGE:
- Deep melancholy and sadness
- Soaring joy and triumph
- Nostalgia and longing
- Romance and tenderness
- Drama and intensity

COMMON PLAYING STYLES:
- Legato (smooth, flowing)
- Staccato (short, detached)
- Pizzicato (plucked)
- Tremolo (rapid bow movement)
- Harmonics (ethereal tones)

SUITED FOR GENRES:
- Classical
- Cinematic/film scores
- Folk (fiddle)
- Indie folk
- Pop (orchestral pop)
- Ambient

TYPICAL MOODS:
- Emotional depth
- Elegant and sophisticated
- Cinematic and epic
- Intimate and vulnerable
- Timeless

COMBINATION INSTRUMENTS:
- Works well with: piano, acoustic guitar, orchestral brass
- Creates lush beds when arranged in sections

CONTEXTUAL USE:
- Emotional climaxes
- Romantic moments
- Nostalgic passages
- Film scores and soundtracks
- Classical compositions
